## Identity rules (VISUAL IDENTITY GUDIELINE - PRINT.pdf, 46 pages, v1.00, 2021 / 1443 AH)

**Logo meaning (p.6):** a film-strip arc ("beyond the horizon") + a red→yellow ball ("passion in animation") + a palm ("Saudi Arabia"). The arc launching the ball is literally a bouncing-ball trajectory, the first exercise every animator learns.

**Structure and clear space (pp.7–9):** the unit X is the ball's diameter. The mark is 6X tall with ½X gaps between the Arabic and English wordmark lines. Clear space is 1.5X on all sides.

**Variants (pp.10–11):**
- Black or white monochrome.
- Single-colour red, yellow, light green and green.
- The full-colour master uses gradients (arc: green→yellow; ball: red→yellow).

**Placement (p.12):** in a page corner.

**Minimum size (p.13):** 130 px digital, 5 cm print (3 cm absolute).

**Misuse (pp.14–15):**
- No resizing or respacing.
- No non-brand or gradient backgrounds, and no photos behind the logo.
- No gradient over the whole logo.
- No poor-contrast colour pairs.
- No dismembering the mark.
- No off-palette colours.

**Primary colours (p.17; also "Saudi Animation Society Colours.png"):**

**Supporting colours (p.18):** a mid blue and a deep navy. The guideline gives no values. Approximate screen samples are blue ≈ #4263A8 and navy ≈ #1B2849; confirm them against the source files before final use.

**Gradients (p.19):** red→yellow (the ball) and green→yellow (the arc).

**Typography (pp.21–23):**
- Logo only: Boahmed AlHarf (Arabic) and Bell Gothic Std (English).
- Arabic: Cairo Black for titles, Cairo Bold for subtitles, Cairo SemiBold for body, Cairo Regular for notes.
- English: RNS Sanz ExtraBold for titles, Bold for subtitles, SemiBold for text, Medium for notes.

**Patterns, icons and supporting elements (pp.25–29):**
- Outlined palm pattern and a gradient film strip.
- 18 gradient line icons (calendar, bezier pen, bulb, music, award, pencil, film reel, gear, microphone, target, tablet, camera, palette, magnifier, video camera, images, rocket).
- Bundles of fine wave lines, dot circles, gradient blobs and topographic contour lines.
- Navy panels with green/yellow sweeps.

**Applications (pp.31–45):** stationery, folder, notepad, business cards, sticker, button, paper bag, key tags, calendar, coaster and mug. The notepad shows the domain "AnimationSociety.org.sa".

## Content facts found (need Society approval before publishing)

**Membership types** ("Highlight- membership.ai" / Story "About us"):
- **Working member (العضو العامل):** founders or specialists. They can attend General Assembly meetings, vote and stand for the board.
- **Affiliate member (العضو المنتسب):** applicants who don't meet the working-member conditions. They join activities and receive updates.
- **Honorary member (العضو الفخري):** granted by General Assembly decision to people who supported the Society.
- **Distinguished member (العضو الشرفي):** granted by the General Assembly; may attend board meetings.

No prices appear anywhere in the Drive.

**Other facts:**
- The letterhead footer carries an official registration line (registration number, decision number and date, Riyadh region). It's a candidate for the site footer, pending approval.
- Domains appear inconsistently: "AnimationSociety.org.sa" on the notepad and "@animation.org.sa" in business-card emails.
